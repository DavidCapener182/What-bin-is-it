What Bin Is It Tonight? gives UK households one clear answer: which bin goes out next?

Add your postcode, choose your exact property and check collection dates returned by a connected council source. The app never creates an estimated schedule and always shows where its information came from.

COLLECTION DAY AT A GLANCE

• See the next verified collection
• View the week ahead
• Keep schedules for your saved places
• See the named source and last successful check
• Get clear cached and unavailable states

REMINDERS THAT FIT BIN NIGHT

• Set a bin-night reminder
• Choose the time and collection types
• Add an optional collection-morning reminder
• Get follow-up, date-change and service-disruption alerts when available

WHAT GOES WHERE?

• Search everyday household items
• See safe preparation guidance
• Check where local rules can differ
• Find nearby recycling centres, recycling points and reuse options
• See accepted materials only when the source declares them

MISSED COLLECTIONS

• Check the collection and eligibility questions
• Open the official council reporting route
• Keep local report history and council references
• Never confuse an app tracking ID with a council report reference

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Saved places, schedules, preferences and local report tracking stay on your device. Location is requested only when you choose it to find a postcode. There are no adverts and the app does not sell address data.

What Bin Is It Tonight? is independent and is not a council service. Collection dates, accepted materials and reporting rules can change, so check the named official source when information is time-critical.
