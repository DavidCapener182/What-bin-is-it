# Commercial product model

## Position

What Bin Is It Tonight? should feel like a trusted public utility, not an advertising product. The launch model is:

1. keep the current resident app free while collection accuracy is proven;
2. introduce Plus only for added convenience;
3. keep essential council information and the basic missed-bin route free;
4. sell measurable resident-service outcomes to councils and property operators;
5. add carefully labelled waste-service referrals only after trust and local accuracy are established.

The live code uses `EXPO_PUBLIC_LAUNCH_PHASE=proof`. Store product identifiers are reserved, but payment prompts remain disabled in this phase.

## Resident plans

### Free

- one saved address;
- verified next collection and schedule;
- standard bin-night reminder;
- bank-holiday and service changes;
- recycling guide and nearby services;
- basic missed-bin route.

### What Bin? Plus

- £1.99 monthly;
- £14.99 yearly, shown as the recommended option;
- optional £29.99 lifetime launch product;
- up to five addresses;
- advanced reminder sequence;
- report history and references;
- calendar subscription;
- household sharing;
- widgets;
- priority support.

Before Plus is activated:

- configure the same product identifiers in App Store Connect and Play Console;
- integrate and validate store billing on physical devices;
- restore purchases;
- add subscription management and cancellation links;
- explain renewal, price, cadence and free functionality on the purchase screen;
- update privacy declarations and review notes;
- do not remove existing essential functionality.

## Councils

| Plan | Annual price | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Starter | £7,500 | Branding, lookup, notifications, guide and links |
| Resident Services | £15,000 | Reporting workflows, disruptions, dashboard, analytics and basic integrations |
| Integrated | £25,000–£40,000 | CRM submission, status, multiple services, custom connectors and service level |

Onboarding: approximately £3,000–£10,000 depending on integration.

## Property operators

- £49 monthly for up to 50 properties;
- £99 monthly for up to 250 properties;
- £249 monthly for larger portfolios;
- enterprise scope for housing associations.

## Holiday lets

A later product can provide a property QR card, guest-friendly instructions, collection schedule, multilingual guidance and host reminders. Indicative pricing is £2 per property monthly with a £10–£15 minimum account. Do not build this before the property workflow has real demand.

## Ethical referrals

Ordering must remain:

1. free council route;
2. local reuse or charity option;
3. council recycling centre;
4. clearly labelled licensed paid partner.

Never use ordinary display advertising, unrelated sponsored notifications, full-screen advertising or the sale of address/location data.
