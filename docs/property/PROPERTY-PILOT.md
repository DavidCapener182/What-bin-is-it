# Property and housing pilot

Property operators can be a faster first contract than a council-wide procurement while testing the same operational value.

## Pilot scope

- 10 to 50 properties or one managed development;
- exact property or communal-bin location;
- collection schedule and service-change alerts;
- caretaker reminder ownership;
- resident share link or QR entry;
- contamination and overflow instructions;
- missed-collection log;
- weekly exception review.

## Discovery information

- property list and collection location, minimized to what is needed;
- individual versus communal service;
- who puts containers out and brings them in;
- recurring contamination, overflow or access issues;
- bulky-waste route;
- caretaker escalation;
- resident languages and accessibility needs;
- existing property-management system;
- data retention and tenancy-change process.

## Success measures

- properties with a verified schedule;
- reminder-task completion;
- recurring missed or overflow events;
- time from failure to responsible-team awareness;
- resident support themes;
- caretaker time saved, using an agreed baseline;
- address and schedule error rate.

## Commercial path

Start with £49 monthly for up to 50 properties. Move to £99 for up to 250 and £249 for larger portfolios only after the multi-property dashboard, role access and exports use authenticated server storage rather than device-only state.

Do not sell the current local-only consumer address store as an enterprise dashboard. The contract phase requires tenancy-aware access, account deletion, audit logging, DPA/DPIA, backup, retention and support ownership.
